Patient-controlled EHR dApp

A GitHub-ready educational dApp for managing healthcare records with encrypted off-chain storage, Ethereum smart-contract access control, DID-style identities, and role-based user flows.

Start registration Open patient dashboard

System flow

  1. Register a patient or healthcare actor.
  2. Verify care providers using the owner/admin account.
  3. Care provider requests access to the patient record.
  4. Patient grants or rejects the request.
  5. Records are encrypted, stored off-chain, and referenced on-chain.
